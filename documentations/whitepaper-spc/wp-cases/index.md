# 8 Business Cases
## Overview
The following business cases illustrate how the SPC platform can create measurable value in
different product configuration scenarios. Whether companies want to unlock additional revenue
from existing PIM data, reduce costs and errors in complex sales processes, or replace outdated
configurator solutions, the approach follows a clear path: consolidate and structure product data,
model configuration logic, enable no-code rule maintenance, validate the solution through a
proof of concept, and finally launch scalable production configurator applications.
Each case demonstrates how guided selling and modern configuration technology can
make product variant selection faster, safer, and more efficient for both customers and sales teams.
