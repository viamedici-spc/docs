## 9.3 Training

### Target Groups

1.  **Key Users (business departments)**\
    In-depth training on [*Rule*](../../../docu-hca/glossary/terms-modeling/#configuration-rule) creation and configuration functions\
    Duration: 2-5 days

2.  **End Users**\
    Short training sessions on advanced configuration functions\
    Duration: 0.5-1 day

### Methods

- In-person training sessions and online webinars

- Interactive workshops for hands-on application
